<h3 align="center">The Radio Intelligence Company 📡🚀</h3>

Qoherent is a Canadian technology startup specializing in AI development for software-defined radios. More information about our company, products, and services can be found on our website at [qoherent.ai](https://www.qoherent.ai/).

## 🛠️ RIA Hub

[RIA Hub](https://www.qoherent.ai/riahub) by Qoherent is an AI development platform for software-defined radios. Leverage the [RIA Toolkit](https://www.qoherent.ai/riatoolkit), along with purpose-built automations, directly in your browser, without the need to write code or set up infrastructure.

## 🤝 Engineering services

Let's build intelligent radios together. We offer prototyping, end-to-end development, and engineering support services. [Contact us](https://qoherent.ai/contact/) to kickstart your next project! 🚀

## 🧰 RIA Toolkit OSS

Check out RIA Toolkit OSS on RIA Hub: [`ria-toolkit-oss`](https://riahub.ai/qoherent/ria-toolkit-oss). RIA Toolkit OSS is **free and open-source**, providing the fundamental components to help engineers and researchers get started building, testing, and deploying radio inference applications.

## 📚 Helpful resources

Explore our public projects, both here and on [RIA Hub](https://riahub.ai)! We're proud to offer a wide range of tutorials showcasing how we apply machine learning to identify things in wireless spectrum.
